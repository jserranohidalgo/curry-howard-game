# Image credits

Every image the game shows, where it came from, and under what licence. Nothing
is fetched while you play: all of it is served from this directory.

## The portraits

| Image | Who | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `curry.jpeg` | Haskell Brooks Curry, 1900–1982 | course material; the same photograph the Ukrainian Wikipedia article carries | **no established licence** — see below |
| `howard.jpg` | William Alvin Howard, May 2004 | [File:William Alvin Howard May 2004.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:William_Alvin_Howard_May_2004.jpg), by **Andrej Bauer**, cropped | **CC BY-SA 2.5** |

| `church.jpg` | Alonzo Church | credited to **Princeton University** | **no established licence** — see below |

The two Creative Commons portraits carry their attribution on the slide that
shows them, as their licences require. Howard's is cropped to the plate and
otherwise unaltered; the crop is offered under the same licence.

**Curry's and Church's photographs have no established licence.** No freely
licensed portrait of either man is known to exist: neither has one on Wikimedia
Commons, and the copies the encyclopaedias use are non-free. They are shown here,
at low resolution, in a free application made for teaching, because the
alternative is two blank plates on the slide that introduces the men the subject
is named after. Church's is credited to Princeton University; the author of
Curry's is unknown.

**If you hold rights in either photograph**, please write to
juanmanuel.serrano@urjc.es. They will be credited as you ask, or removed.

## The marks

The game's own mark — the X, the lambda, the turnstile and the discs they reduce
to — was drawn for this project and carries no third-party rights.

`logo-etsii.svg` is the lockup of the Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería
Informática at the Universidad Rey Juan Carlos, shown in the *About* panel. This
is not an official application of the university.
